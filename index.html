Product Concept Overview

What is BioSense Sphere?

BioSense Sphere is a proposed compact biosensing platform designed around a small sensing sphere containing multiple sensing components. The concept is to detect multiple biological indicators and convert their responses into measurable signals that can be interpreted by an external reader and software system.

Current Stage: Concept / Prototype Development
Architecture 01

MULTIPARAMETER

"Designed to monitor multiple biological signals."

Engineered as a multi-modal platform capable of integrating distinct responsive mechanisms rather than restricting analysis to a single biomarker.

Architecture 02

COMPACT

"A small-form-factor sensing architecture designed for integration into wearable or biomedical systems."

Miniaturized geometry designed for low footprint, minimal physical interference, and transient biocompatible materials.

Architecture 03

SMART

"Designed to translate biochemical responses into digital information for interpretation."

Pairs transient sensing consumables with an external wireless reader and digital algorithm pipeline for timely decision support.

Core Mechanism

How BioSense Sphere Works

A comprehensive, step-by-step translation from localized biological changes to clear digital insight.

STEP 01

Biological Signal

The surrounding biological environment contains measurable biochemical or physiological changes (e.g. localized pH shifts, thermal fluctuations, enzyme expression).

STEP 02

Selective Sensing

Specialized sensing materials interact with selected targets such as pH changes, enzymes, proteins, or other biomarkers depending on the intended application.

STEP 03

Signal Transduction

The biochemical interaction produces a measurable physical, electrochemical, or optical response within the miniaturized sphere architecture.

STEP 04

External Reader

A reusable external reader noninvasively detects or measures the transduced signal from the sensing element via near-field or wireless link.

STEP 05

Digital Analysis

The measured raw sensor values are filtered, processed, and converted into an interpretable digital output and normalized metrics.

STEP 06

Actionable Insight

The system provides understandable information about detected biological changes to healthcare professionals and monitoring systems.

Important Research Notice: The exact sensing architecture, target biomarker configurations, materials, and readout circuitry are currently undergoing benchtop research and iterative prototype development.

Engineering & Biosensing Concepts

Our Technology

Core scientific concepts underpinning the BioSense Sphere prototype development.

Multiparameter Biosensing

Enabling concurrent tracking of diverse physical and chemical markers to reduce false indications and provide richer biological context.

Biomarker Detection

Targeted receptor interfaces sensitive to early biochemical shifts such as localized enzyme activity, pH alterations, and inflammation markers.

Signal Transduction

Translating biological binding events and chemical gradients into measurable electromagnetic or electrochemical signals.

Miniaturized Sensing

Compact spherical geometry engineered for localized placement without bulky mechanical housings or patient discomfort.

External Signal Reading

Near-field telemetry pairs transient consumables with a durable, reusable reader device, significantly lowering per-patient expense.

Digital Interpretation

Signal-processing algorithms designed to filter background noise and translate raw sensor voltages into clear risk parameters.

Modular Sensing Architecture

Designed as a flexible platform that allows different chemistry layers to be customized for specific monitoring environments (e.g. post-operative wounds, burn care, or chronic ulcers).

* Note: The final combination of sensing materials, transduction methods, electronics, and algorithms is actively being optimized in the lab.
Target Analyte Scope

What Can It Detect?

Potential sensing targets under investigation for early biochemical indication.

ANALYTE 01

Localized pH Shifts

Monitoring microenvironment acidity/alkalinity gradients associated with bacterial colonization and tissue repair phases.

ANALYTE 02

Localized Temperature

Detecting micro-thermal elevations characteristic of hyper-vascularization and localized inflammatory responses.

ANALYTE 03

Enzyme / Protease Activity

Tracking elevated protease concentrations (such as matrix metalloproteinases) linked to extracellular matrix breakdown.

ANALYTE 04

Protein Biomarkers

Investigating receptor-specific recognition elements for targeted biological indicators (e.g. MMP-9, IL-6).

ANALYTE 05

Inflammatory Biomarkers

Identifying localized cytokine and biochemical cues that precede macroscopic redness and systemic fever.

ANALYTE 06

Other Biochemical Indicators

Modular architecture allows exploration of additional metabolic and electrolytic markers as research advances.

What We Are Building

Our active prototype engineering efforts are focused on six key technical domains:

Physical Architecture

Designing miniaturized geometries that provide optimal surface-area-to-volume ratio for analyte exposure.

Sensing Materials

Screening biocompatible polymers, functionalized coatings, and responsive chemical matrices.

Signal Detection

Quantifying signal-to-noise ratios (SNR), sensitivity, and reproducibility under benchtop conditions.

Reader Integration

Developing low-noise signal acquisition electronics and near-field wireless telemetry hardware.

Prototype Testing

Executing in-vitro benchtop experiments to validate material degradation profiles and sensor kinetics.

Data Interpretation

Constructing algorithms to translate analog multi-sensor data streams into clear diagnostic cues.

Prospective Horizons

Potential Applications

Investigating target use cases where compact, multiparameter biochemical sensing can create significant clinical and diagnostic value.

Monitoring surgical incisions and closed sites for subtle biochemical markers indicative of early surgical site complications.
